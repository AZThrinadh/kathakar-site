Kathakar privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What Kathakar is

Kathakar is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It makes videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not a public service.

Google and YouTube data

When the owner connects YouTube, Kathakar asks Google for permission to upload videos and to read the channel name. It uses that permission only to upload videos the owner made and to confirm which channel is connected.

  • The sign-in key from Google is stored only in a settings file on the owner's computer.
  • Kathakar does not send Google or YouTube data to any other server or person.
  • Kathakar does not sell, share or use this data for advertising.
  • Kathakar's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the settings file on the computer also removes the stored key.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/AZThrinadh/kathakar-site/issues.
